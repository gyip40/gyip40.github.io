01About me

A little more about me

I'm a Computer Science and Applied Math & Statistics student at Stony Brook University with a minor in Linguistics, interested in the intersection between technology and the humanities in fields like AI and NLP. I'm currently pursuing a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

I'm drawn to projects that sit between code and the real world: a ResNet-50 classifier that spots leukemia cells with explainable heatmaps, a blockchain loan-verification platform pitched to Broadridge executives, and access-control logic for a volunteer platform at ReWild Long Island.

I love mentoring: I've previously served as an Undergraduate TA for CSE 150 (Foundations of Computer Science), where I taught 30+ students to instill good logic and mathematical practices. On campus, I am part of Stony Brook's Women in Computer Science (WiCS) E-Board as the Event Committee Chair, where I oversee all events held for the 2026-2027 school year, including a speaker panel with GEICO, a full-day networking convention with women in the tech field, and our annual hackathon, HopperHacks. As an Event Committee Chair, I also manage the WiCS Mentorship Program, which pairs underclassmen with helpful mentors in academic, social, and professional matters. Additionally, I am part of the Mahjong Club, where my role as the Vice-President is to help with logistics and serve as the connection between members and club leaders.
